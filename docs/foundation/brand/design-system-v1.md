# Design System: "The Executive Presence"

- **Tier:** Foundation
- **Status:** LOCKED
- **Version:** v1
- **Source:** Kdenz Master project brief - Google Docs.pdf

---

## Design Philosophy

"High-Performance Clinical" -- Peloton energy meets Apple Health precision.

- Mode: Light only (no dark mode for beta)
- Clean, professional, data-forward
- Communicates competence and trust

| Accent | Electric Teal/Cyan | `#00D4FF` | Highlights, progress indicators, active states |

## Typography

| Element | Font | Weight | Size |
|---------|------|--------|------|
| Headlines | Inter | Bold (700) | 24-32px |
| Body | Inter | Regular (400) | 16px |
| Data / Metrics | SF Mono / JetBrains Mono | Medium (500) | 14-18px |
| Labels | Inter | Medium (500) | 12-14px |

## Design Principles

1. **Data-first:** Metrics are the hero -- large, readable, baseline-compared
2. **No judgment in visuals:** Colors indicate change direction, not good/bad
3. **Progressive disclosure:** Summary first, details on demand
4. **Accessibility:** WCAG AA minimum contrast ratios
5. **Consistency:** Same metric always looks the same across all views
