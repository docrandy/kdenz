# Rewrite Recipes: Turning Risky/Forbidden Copy Into Safe Copy

- **Tier:** Foundation
- **Status:** LOCKED
- **Version:** v1
- **Source:** Claim-Safe Copy Library and SaaS Action Plan (original docx)
- **Summary:** Four patterns for rewriting risky or forbidden claims into safe, descriptive, user-empowering language.

---

## Pattern 1: Replace mental-state labels with measurements

**Instead of:** "You sounded nervous."
**Use:** "Your pace increased by {delta_wpm} WPM vs. baseline (+/-{wpm_ci}). What was happening for you in that moment?"

---

## Pattern 2: Use modal verbs and invite user interpretation

**Instead of:** "You rushed."
**Use:** "A {delta_wpm} WPM increase occurred in this section. What do you think caused the shift?"

---

## Pattern 3: Blame the system's limits, not the user

**Instead of:** "You hesitated too much."
**Use:** "Pause detection flagged {pause_over_2s} pauses >2s. Pauses can reflect thinking, emphasis, or speech style. Does this match your perception?"

---

## Pattern 4: Avoid prescriptive norms; offer choices

**Instead of:** "Aim for 140-150 WPM."
**Use:** "Your pace varied between {min_wpm}-{max_wpm}. If you want a calmer feel, you can experiment with slightly longer pauses and see how it lands with your audience."
