# Audio Quality and Failure Mode Warning Messages

- **Tier:** Foundation
- **Status:** LOCKED
- **Version:** v1
- **Source:** Claim-Safe Copy Library and SaaS Action Plan (original docx)
- **Summary:** Standard warning messages displayed when audio quality issues affect metric reliability.

---

## Warning Templates

- **Background noise:** "Background noise detected; pause metrics may be inaccurate."
- **Low transcription confidence:** "Low transcription confidence in this segment; filler counts may be under- or over-estimated."
- **Overlapping speakers:** "Overlapping speakers detected; turn-taking and pause metrics may be unreliable."
- **Microphone clipping:** "Microphone clipping detected; pitch/volume estimates may be distorted."
