---

## User Controls

- Disable speech analysis (no penalty).
- Granular controls:
  - Skip filler detection
  - Skip pause analysis
  - Skip pitch analysis
  - Skip all speech analysis
- Turn speech analysis back on anytime.
- Deleting audio does not remove your coaching notes or transcripts unless you choose to delete those too.

---

## Experimental Feature Disclosure (If Emotion/Confidence Detection Is Ever Tested)

**Recommended default: do not offer this feature in non-clinical coaching.**

If tested despite recommendation:
- Label: EXPERIMENTAL -- Low accuracy
- Disclosure: This feature may misclassify speech and has known bias, especially for non-native speakers and some demographics.
- Consent: Separate opt-in (you can opt out without losing access to other features).
- Display: Show wide confidence ranges (e.g., +/-20 percentage points).
