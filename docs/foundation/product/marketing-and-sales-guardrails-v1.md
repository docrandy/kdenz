# Marketing and Sales Copy Guardrails

- **Tier:** Foundation
- **Status:** LOCKED
- **Version:** v1
- **Source:** Claim-Safe Copy Library and SaaS Action Plan (original docx)
- **Summary:** Allowed positioning statements and statements to avoid in all marketing, sales, and external-facing content.

---

## Allowed Positioning Statements

- "Tracks speaking pace, pauses, filler words, and pitch range to support practice and reflection."
- "Highlights changes compared to your personal baseline over time."
- "Provides descriptive feedback; you interpret what it means in context."
- "Shows uncertainty and audio-quality limitations so you know when metrics are less reliable."

---

## Statements to Avoid (and Safer Rewrites)

| Avoid | Rewrite |
|-------|---------|
| "Detects anxiety." | "Shows measurable changes (pace, pauses, pitch range) that may vary with context; you decide what's true for you." |
| "Increases confidence." | "Helps users practice and reflect on communication patterns." |
| "Improves communication by X%." | "Users can track their own trends over sessions." |
| "Tells you how professional you sound." | "Provides counts and rates (e.g., fillers, pace) without judging 'good/bad.'" |
