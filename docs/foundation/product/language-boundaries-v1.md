# Language Boundaries

- **Tier:** Foundation
- **Status:** LOCKED
- **Version:** v1

- **Summary:** Hard-banned words and phrases that must never appear in the product, marketing, or communications. Non-negotiable language constraints.

---

## Hard-Banned Words and Phrases

These words/phrases are **permanently forbidden** in all contexts -- product UI, marketing, sales, support, and documentation.

### Diagnostic / Clinical Terms

| Banned Term | Why | Use Instead |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| "diagnose" / "diagnosis" | Implies clinical authority | "identify patterns", "track metrics" |
| "disorder" | Medical classification | Never needed |
| "therapy" / "therapeutic" | Clinical treatment framing | "practice", "coaching" |
| "patient" | Clinical relationship | "user", "coachee" |
| "treatment" | Medical intervention | "practice plan", "approach" |
| "symptom" | Medical framing | "pattern", "observation" |

### Psychological Attribution

| Banned Term | Why | Use Instead |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| "anxious" / "anxiety" | Psychological diagnosis from audio | "your pace increased" |
| "confident" / "confidence score" | Subjective internal state | "your pitch range was X semitones" |
| "nervous" | Emotional attribution | "speech rate was higher than your baseline" |
| "stressed" | Internal state inference | Describe observable metrics only |
| "emotional state" | Cannot be measured from audio alone | "vocal characteristics" |

### Judgment / Prescriptive

| Banned Term | Why | Use Instead |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| "good" / "bad" (about speech) | Subjective evaluation | "compared to your baseline" |
| "correct" / "incorrect" | Implies universal standard | "typical for you" vs "different from your baseline" |
| "improve" (as imperative) | Prescriptive | "you may want to explore" |
| "you need to" | Prescriptive, judgment | "you might consider" |
| "weakness" | Deficit framing | "area of change" |
| "problem" (about user speech) | Deficit framing | "pattern", "observation" |
| "normal" / "abnormal" | Implies population norms | "your typical range" |

### Accuracy / Certainty Overreach

| Banned Term | Why | Use Instead |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| "accurately detects" | Overpromises | "tracks", "estimates" |
| "proven to" | Requires clinical evidence | "research suggests", "designed to" |
| "scientifically validated" | Unless independently verified | "evidence-informed" |
| "AI-powered insights" | Vague, overpromises | Name the specific metric |

## Enforcement

- Run all copy through claim taxonomy check before publication
- See `foundation/copy/claim-taxonomy-safe-risky-forbidden-v1.md` for full classification system
- When in doubt, describe the measurement, not the meaning
