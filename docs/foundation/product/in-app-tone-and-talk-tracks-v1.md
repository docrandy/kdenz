# In-App Tone and Talk Tracks

- **Tier:** Foundation
- **Status:** LOCKED
- **Version:** v1
- **Source:** Claim-Safe Copy Library and SaaS Action Plan (original docx)
- **Summary:** How the product should sound in-app, in sales conversations, and in support responses. Defines the communication voice.

---

## In-App Tone (What the Product Should Sound Like)

1. "Here's what we measured." (counts/rates/ranges)
2. "Here's how it compares to your baseline." (delta)
3. "Here's how confident the system is." (+/- error / audio quality)
4. "Here are a few reasons this might vary." (context options, not conclusions)
5. "What do you think was happening?" (user reflection)

---

## Sales Pitch (Safe, Defensible)

- "We provide descriptive speech analytics (pace, pauses, fillers, pitch range) designed for practice and reflection."
- "We don't claim to detect emotions or mental states; that's unreliable and can be biased."
- "Our feedback is baseline-relative and includes uncertainty indicators so teams can trust when it's strong vs. noisy."

---

## Support Responses: When Users Ask "Am I Anxious / Confident?"

- "We can't measure anxiety or confidence from voice alone. What we can show are changes in pace, pauses, and pitch range."
- "If you tell us what you were feeling, we can help you interpret the metrics and adjust your practice goals."
