# VoiceLab Beta Testing - Welcome!

Thanks for helping test VoiceLab! This is an early version of a voice practice app that helps you discover unconscious speaking habits.

---

## Getting Started

**Production URL:** https://kdenz.vercel.app

**Requirements:**
- **Chrome browser only** (desktop or mobile)
- Safari/Firefox have 90% accuracy drop in voice recognition - not worth the frustration

**Quick Start:**
1. Open the URL in Chrome
2. Allow microphone access when prompted
3. Pick a focus mode (filler words or pace)
4. Talk for 1-3 minutes about anything
5. Review your stats and listen to playback

---

## What to Try

**Filler Word Mode:**
- Detects "um", "uh", "like", "you know", etc.
- Real-time gauge shows filler rate
- Audio playback highlights exactly when you said them

**Pace Mode:**
- Tracks words per minute
- Shows if you're too fast, too slow, or in the sweet spot
- Helps you find your natural rhythm

**AI Summary (if configured):**
- Get insights from Gemini about your speaking patterns
- Only works if API key is configured (gracefully skips if not)

**Audio Playback:**
- Listen to your recording
- See filler words highlighted in transcript
- The "aha moment" feature - most people don't realize their patterns until they hear themselves

## Known Limitations

This is a beta MVP - some things aren't built yet:

- **Chrome only** - Safari/Firefox won't work reliably
- **No session history** - your data clears when you close the browser (by design for privacy, but history feature coming later)
- **Local storage only** - nothing is saved to a server
- **Basic stats** - advanced metrics coming in future versions
