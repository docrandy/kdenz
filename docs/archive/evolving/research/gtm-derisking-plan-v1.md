## 8 Assumptions to Stop Using Immediately

These are commonly repeated but unvalidated. Stop treating them as facts:

1. "Everyone wants to sound more confident" -- confidence is subjective and culturally variable
2. "Filler words are always bad" -- context-dependent, some audiences prefer natural speech
3. "Faster = more confident" -- could indicate anxiety, cultural norm, or neurodivergence
4. "Users will self-motivate to practice weekly" -- retention data from competitors suggests otherwise
5. "B2B is always better than B2C" -- depends on sales cycle, decision-maker access
6. "AI coaching can replace human coaching" -- different value propositions
7. "More metrics = more value" -- overwhelming metrics is the #1 UX complaint in competitor reviews
8. "Job seekers and managers want the same thing" -- different urgency, context, and outcomes

## Interview Methodology

### 30-Minute Interview Script

**Opening (5 min):**
- "Tell me about the last time you had an important speaking moment -- interview, presentation, meeting."
- "How did you feel about your performance?"
- "What, if anything, did you do to prepare?"

**Pain Discovery (10 min):**
- "What's the hardest part about speaking in professional settings?"
- "Have you ever noticed habits in your speech you wanted to change?"
- "What have you tried before? What worked? What didn't?"

**Solution Testing (10 min):**
- Show concept: "Imagine an app that records you speaking and shows you exactly how many filler words you used, your pace, and how it compares to your own baseline."
- "What's your reaction to that?"
- "Would this be more useful before an important event, or as ongoing practice?"
- "What would make you come back to this weekly?"

**Willingness to Pay (5 min):**
- "If this existed today, what would you expect to pay?"
- "At $10-15/month, would you subscribe? For how long?"
- "What would make this a no-brainer purchase?"

## B2B Discovery Track

Run in parallel with consumer interviews:

1. **Identify 5-10 L&D decision-makers** at mid-size companies (200-2000 employees)
2. **Key questions:**
   - How do you currently train communication skills?
   - What's your budget per employee for soft skill development?
   - Would you pilot a tool that gives employees objective speech feedback?
   - What would make you approve a per-seat purchase ($8-15/seat/month)?
3. **Signals to track:**
   - Interest level (1-5 scale based on conversation)
   - Budget authority and cycle
   - Current tools in use (and satisfaction)

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|---------|
| Free | $0 | 1 recording/day, basic metrics (pace, fillers) |
| Individual Premium | $10-15/mo | Unlimited recordings, all metrics, trends, playback |
| Team (B2B) | $8-12/seat/mo | Premium + admin dashboard, team analytics |
| Coaching Add-on | $50-100/mo | Premium + human coaching check-ins |

## Research Traps to Avoid

1. **Confirmation bias:** Don't lead with "Would you use an app that helps you speak better?" (everyone says yes)
2. **Hypothetical vs actual behavior:** "Would you pay?" is less reliable than "What have you actually paid for?"
3. **Small sample overfitting:** Don't pivot strategy based on 2-3 interviews
4. **Feature creep from interviews:** Users will ask for everything -- stay focused on core value
5. **Ignoring negative signals:** If 7/10 say "meh," that's a signal, not noise
