## Situation

Multiple assumptions driving product and go-to-market strategy remain **unvalidated**. This plan de-risks the most critical ones in 14 days for under $1,000.

## Six Unvalidated Assumptions

| # | Assumption | Risk if Wrong | Priority |
|---|-----------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | Job seekers will pay $10-15/mo for speech coaching | No revenue model | CRITICAL |
| 2 | Real-time filler feedback creates the "holy shit moment" | No activation hook | CRITICAL |
| 3 | Users will complete a baseline session before getting value | No onboarding completion | HIGH |
| 4 | Early-career managers are a viable secondary ICP | Wrong expansion path | HIGH |
| 5 | Freemium converts at 20%+ to paid | Unsustainable unit economics | MEDIUM |
| 6 | Users practice weekly without external accountability | No retention | MEDIUM |

## 14-Day Plan: 5 Decisions to Make

| Decision | Method | By Day |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Primary ICP (job seekers vs managers) | 10-20 interviews per segment | Day 7 |
| Willingness to pay ($10-15/mo threshold) | Interview + survey | Day 7 |
| Core feature priority (real-time vs playback) | Prototype testing | Day 10 |
| Messaging that resonates | A/B test 3 positioning variants | Day 12 |
| Kill/continue decision | Aggregate evidence review | Day 14 |

## Budget

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| User research incentives ($25/interview x 30) | $750 |
| Survey tool (Typeform or similar) | $0-50 |
| Prototype hosting | $0 (Vercel free tier) |
| Contingency | $199 |
| **Total** | **$999 max** |

## Kill Criteria

Stop building if ANY of these are true after 14 days:

1. Fewer than 3/10 interviewees say they would pay $10/mo
2. Zero interviewees describe filler awareness as valuable
3. Both ICP segments show low urgency (no time pressure to improve)
4. Competitive alternatives are "good enough" for target users
