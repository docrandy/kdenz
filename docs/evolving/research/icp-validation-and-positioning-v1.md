## ICP Comparison

### Early-Career Managers
- **Pain:** Leading meetings, giving feedback, presenting to leadership
- **Willingness to pay:** Moderate (may expense through employer)
- **Volume:** Large segment, growing
- **Urgency:** Medium (ongoing development, not crisis-driven)
- **B2B potential:** High (L&D budgets, per-seat licensing)

### Job Seekers (Interview Prep)
- **Pain:** Interview performance, first impressions, articulation under pressure
- **Willingness to pay:** High (time-bounded, high-stakes)
- **Volume:** Very large, seasonal spikes
- **Urgency:** High (interview dates create natural deadlines)
- **B2B potential:** Low (individual purchase)

## Positioning Strategy Options

1. **Interview-prep first:** Highest urgency + WTP, clearest use case, fastest validation
2. **Manager-first:** Better B2B path, higher LTV, but harder to validate quickly
3. **Both with segmented onboarding:** More work upfront, but captures both segments

## Research Needed

- 10-20 interviews per segment to validate pain intensity and WTP
- Test messaging variants per segment
- Identify which segment has faster activation (first "holy shit moment")
