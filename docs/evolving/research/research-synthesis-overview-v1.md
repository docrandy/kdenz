## 7 Key Strategic Insights

1. **Unique competitive advantage:** Clinical expertise + volitional systems + ethical AI transparency. Don't compete on features with Orai/Yoodli -- compete on philosophy.
2. **Emotion recognition is a trap:** Biased against women, non-native speakers, neurodivergent users. Focus on less-biased metrics (pacing, fillers, pauses).
3. **Market is real but fragmented:** Job seekers have highest intent + WTP. Start there, expand to presenters -> creators -> non-native speakers.
4. **Freemium is the right model:** Free tier builds habit; premium ($10-15/mo) unlocks all metrics + trends; coaching add-on ($50-100/mo) for human verification.
5. **Real-time feedback wins:** Post-hoc analysis is easier but less engaging. Real-time pacing gauge + filler alerts drive engagement.
6. **Gamification works but can backfire:** Streaks and progress charts yes; global leaderboards and judgment language no. Personalized benchmarks, effort-based rewards.
7. **Behavior change psychology is core:** GAIN framework, transparency about limitations, volitional diagnostics as genuine differentiator.
