## CRITICAL Priority

### 1. ICP Validation (Job Seekers vs Managers)
- **Question:** Which segment has higher urgency, WTP, and activation potential?
- **Hypothesis:** Job seekers have higher urgency due to time-bounded events (interviews)
- **Method:** 10-20 interviews per segment
- **Success Metric:** Clear winner on urgency + WTP dimensions
- **Kill Criteria:** Neither segment shows strong urgency or WTP
- **Time:** 1-2 weeks | **Cost:** $500-750

### 2. Willingness to Pay ($10-15/mo)
- **Question:** Will target users pay $10-15/month for speech coaching?
- **Hypothesis:** 30%+ of job seekers would pay during active job search
- **Method:** Interview + survey with pricing ladder
- **Success Metric:** 3+ out of 10 interviewees say "yes" at $10/mo
- **Kill Criteria:** Fewer than 3/10 willing to pay
- **Time:** 1 week | **Cost:** $250-500

### 3. Core Value Proposition Resonance
- **Question:** Does "discover unconscious speaking habits" resonate more than "improve your speaking"?
- **Hypothesis:** Discovery/awareness framing beats improvement framing
- **Method:** A/B messaging test (landing page or ad variants)
- **Success Metric:** 2x click-through on discovery framing
- **Kill Criteria:** No significant difference between framings
- **Time:** 1-2 weeks | **Cost:** $100-200

## HIGH Priority

### 4. Baseline Session Completion
- **Question:** Will users complete a 3-5 minute baseline recording before getting value?
- **Hypothesis:** 60%+ will complete if framed as "discover your patterns"
- **Method:** Prototype test with 10-15 users
- **Success Metric:** 60%+ baseline completion rate
- **Kill Criteria:** Under 40% completion
- **Time:** 1-2 weeks | **Cost:** $200

### 5. Real-Time vs Post-Session Preference
- **Question:** Do users prefer real-time feedback (gauge) or post-session review (playback)?
- **Hypothesis:** Both are valued, but real-time drives engagement
- **Method:** Split prototype test
- **Success Metric:** Clear preference signal (60%+ for one mode)
- **Kill Criteria:** N/A (both may be needed)
- **Time:** 2 weeks | **Cost:** $200

### 6. Filler Word Awareness Impact
- **Question:** Does showing filler counts actually change behavior?
- **Hypothesis:** Awareness alone reduces fillers 40%+ within 3 sessions
- **Method:** Pre/post measurement across 10 users
- **Success Metric:** Measurable reduction in filler rate
- **Kill Criteria:** No change after 5 sessions
- **Time:** 3-4 weeks | **Cost:** $200-400

### 7. Retention Without External Accountability
- **Question:** Will users return weekly without a coach or social pressure?
- **Hypothesis:** 30%+ weekly retention for first month
- **Method:** Beta cohort tracking (30+ users, 4 weeks)
- **Success Metric:** 30%+ return in week 4
- **Kill Criteria:** Under 15% return by week 3
- **Time:** 4 weeks | **Cost:** $0 (built into beta)

## MEDIUM Priority

### 8. B2B Per-Seat Pricing Viability
- **Question:** Will L&D buyers pay $8-12/seat/month?
- **Hypothesis:** Competitive with existing communication training ($50-200/person/year)
- **Method:** 5-10 L&D decision-maker interviews
- **Success Metric:** 2+ express strong interest in piloting
- **Kill Criteria:** Zero interest after 10 conversations
- **Time:** 2-3 weeks | **Cost:** $0

### 9. Freemium Conversion Rate
- **Question:** What free-to-paid conversion rate is realistic?
- **Hypothesis:** 15-25% (based on Orai/Yoodli benchmarks)
- **Method:** Launch free tier, track conversion
- **Success Metric:** 15%+ conversion within 30 days
- **Kill Criteria:** Under 5% after 60 days
- **Time:** 2-3 months | **Cost:** $0

### 10. Onboarding Diagnostic Value
- **Question:** Does the 3-5 question volitional diagnostic improve activation?
- **Hypothesis:** Users who complete diagnostic have 2x higher week-2 retention
- **Method:** A/B test: with vs without diagnostic in onboarding
- **Success Metric:** Measurable retention lift from diagnostic group
- **Kill Criteria:** No difference between groups
- **Time:** 4-6 weeks | **Cost:** $0

### 11. Competitive Differentiation Perception
- **Question:** Can users articulate what makes Kdenz different from Yoodli/Orai?
- **Hypothesis:** Ethical transparency + volitional diagnostics are perceived differentiators
- **Method:** Concept testing with competitor-aware users
- **Success Metric:** Users can articulate 1+ unique value prop
- **Kill Criteria:** Users see no difference
- **Time:** 2 weeks | **Cost:** $200

## LOW Priority

### 12. Non-Native Speaker Interest
- **Question:** Is accent/pronunciation coaching a viable expansion?
- **Hypothesis:** Large market but different product requirements
- **Method:** Exploratory interviews with 5-10 non-native speakers
- **Success Metric:** Signal of interest and unmet need
- **Kill Criteria:** N/A (expansion research)
- **Time:** 2 weeks | **Cost:** $100

### 13. Age Segment Viability
- **Question:** Do users 45+ have different needs and WTP?
- **Hypothesis:** Similar needs, potentially higher WTP for career advancement
- **Method:** Include age diversity in interview pool
- **Success Metric:** No significant age-based barriers
- **Kill Criteria:** N/A
- **Time:** Embedded in other research | **Cost:** $0

### 14. Gamification Impact on Retention
- **Question:** Do streaks/progress charts meaningfully improve retention?
- **Hypothesis:** 10-15% retention lift (Duolingo benchmark: 12%)
- **Method:** A/B test post-launch
- **Success Metric:** Measurable retention lift
- **Kill Criteria:** N/A
- **Time:** 4-6 weeks post-launch | **Cost:** $0

### 15. Content Marketing Viability
- **Question:** Can organic content drive acquisition at <$5 CAC?
- **Hypothesis:** Interview tips content has high search volume and conversion intent
- **Method:** Publish 5-10 SEO-optimized articles, track signups
- **Success Metric:** 100+ signups from organic in first month
- **Kill Criteria:** Under 20 signups after 2 months
- **Time:** 2-3 months | **Cost:** $0-200
