- Feeling pill "Calm & safe" resolves via inline disambiguation rather than showing archetype names directly — preserves the "diagnose internally" principle from CLAUDE.md

- Gap awareness (Q3) is stored for coaching context only — does not affect archetype selection
- 2-second auto-dismiss on confirmation card before calling onComplete()
- ProfilePage reads `getAspiration()` fresh at render time (not props) — always shows current state
