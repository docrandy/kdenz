## Decisions Made

- **ExtendedStateObject via ref, not useState:** The extended state is written per-exchange but consumed by future plans (Gemini Call 1 for character AI responses), not rendered in JSX. Using useRef avoids unnecessary re-renders and the "declared but never read" TypeScript error.
- **Panel B positioning:** Placed between CriteriaBar and chat messages area — visible above the conversation flow, below the scoring criteria.
- **Session pattern gate:** 3+ exchanges required, 60% dominance ratio across non-appropriate exchanges, 0.75 average confidence required. This prevents premature pattern labeling from 1-2 exchanges.
- **Regex-first design:** 6 regex patterns cover hedger, presumptuous, fixer/rusher, explainer patterns instantly. Gemini adds nuanced classification for intellectualizing, deflection, validation_seeking that regex can't detect reliably.
