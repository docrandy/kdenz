## Decisions Made

- Skill gaps and VCM gate failures are present in LearnerProfile as typed empty arrays — Phase 21 (mastery tracking) and Phase 24 (VCM diagnostics) will populate them. No placeholder logic needed now.
- Pattern confidence thresholds: high = >= 70% consistent pattern across >= 5 sessions; medium = >= 40% consistent or >= 3 sessions total; low = everything else.
- inst-009 (exercise) is debrief_eligible: false — exercises require setup context and shouldn't surface in the post-session moment.
- Score > 0 required for inclusion in getTopContent() results. Items that only receive penalties (consumed, dismissed, unmet prereqs) are fully suppressed.
- Scoring tie-breaking: score desc first, then tier asc (lower tier surfaces before advanced content on equal scores).
