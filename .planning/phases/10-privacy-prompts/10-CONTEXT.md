# Phase 10: Privacy & Prompts - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-04
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Privacy transparency page at /privacy and 1-2 optional speaking prompts available before sessions. The privacy page builds trust by explaining what the app does and doesn't do with user data. Speaking prompts give users something to talk about during practice.

Privacy page and simple speaking prompts are in scope. Full educational content, tutorials, knowledge sections, and theory material are deferred to a future version.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Privacy page tone
- Warm and transparent — friendly language explaining exactly what happens with data
- Not a legal document — a trust-building page for friends/family beta testers
- Key points: no emotion detection, local processing, what Gemini sees (if AI summary used)

### Speaking prompts
- Simple suggestions to give users something to talk about (e.g., "Describe your morning routine", "Talk about a recent experience")
- Claude's discretion on placement in the flow and exact prompts

### Claude's Discretion
- Speaking prompt placement (pre-session screen, session page, or both)
- Prompt content (1-2 simple speaking topics)
- Privacy page layout and content structure
- How to make privacy page discoverable (footer link, settings, etc.)

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Privacy page should make beta testers feel safe using the app
- Speaking prompts are simple conversation starters, not structured exercises

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- **Educational content / knowledge section** — Full tutorials on communication skills (e.g., "Labeling the Emotions" theory + practice). User envisions a knowledge hub with theory, tutorials, and then practice sessions with tips. This is a significant feature that belongs in a future version (v1.1+), not the MVP.
- **In-session tips/coaching prompts** — Option to get a tip or coaching cue during active practice. Deferred to post-MVP.

</deferred>
